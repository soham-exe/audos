One library for what you own and what you find.

Seamless Listening

Stream smoothly, download instantly, and build your library without interruptions.

  • Instant Playback. Stream your favorite tracks seamlessly without buffering delays or manual setups.
  • Auto-downloads. Let Audos automatically download tracks in the background as you listen, ensuring everything feels like a unified library.
  • Radio mode. When the queue runs out, Audos finds related tracks and keeps going seamlessly.

Library Management

Point it at a folder and it does the rest.

  • Scans local folders. MP3, FLAC, M4A, WAV, OGG, Opus, AAC and WMA, read recursively.
  • Reads your tags. Title, artist, album and embedded cover art.
  • Keeps itself tidy. New files appear, deleted files disappear dynamically.

Playback

Built for keeping a session moving.

  • A real queue. Drag to reorder, drag to append, click to jump.
  • Shuffle, repeat and volume with repeat off, all or one.
  • Keyboard shortcuts. Space plays and pauses, Ctrl+←/→ skips, Ctrl+/ focuses search.

Interface

Seven polished themes, and it's one CSS file to add yours.

  • Beautifully styled. Switch between Paper, Midnight, Terminal, Glacier, Sakura, Deep Forest, and Nightbloom directly from the profile menu.
  • Make it yours. Use any image or video as the backdrop, and any picture as your avatar.
  • Fast at any size. A local SQLite database and a virtualized track list keep large libraries instant.
Audos collects nothing.

No accounts, no analytics, no phone-home. Your library lives in a database on your machine. The only outbound traffic is YouTube when you search or stream, and GitHub for updates, which are signed and verified before they install.

Before you install

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • Minimal disk space footprint for your local library.
  • An internet connection for streaming and updates.
  • SmartScreen may warn on first run. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  • macOS and Linux builds aren't available yet.

First launch

  1. Audos opens instantly, ready to play your music.
  2. Click ➕ in the sidebar and pick a music folder.
  3. Search for anything. Your library comes first, then YouTube.
  4. Right-click the profile button for themes, download mode and backgrounds.
